Course Planner Agent

For professors and staff. This tool helps you do two things: (1) see which weeks of your semester are overloaded for students, and (2) see which students need a check-in, without tallying anything by hand. Everything runs in your browser, so nothing you type is uploaded or saved. The sample course and students below are made up.

Step 1About your course

Step 2Add your graded work

Add each quiz, paper, lab or exam one at a time. "Week" counts from the first Monday (Week 1 is the week that starts on it).

Step 1What has been due so far?

Type the names of the assignments that have already come due, separated by commas.

Step 2Who is in your class?

Type one student per line. Use names only if your institution allows it; initials or ID numbers also work. These names are made up.

Step 3Mark each student's work

Tap a button to switch it between Done, Late and Missing. The results below update right away.

How to read the results

Heavy week means a large share of the grade (15% or more) or three or more items are due in the same week. Reach out first means a student has two or more missing assignments. Keep an eye on means one missing or two or more late. These are suggestions only; you decide what to move and who to contact. Use real student information only in line with your institution's privacy policy (FERPA).
